— About the studio

Six people, one workshop, no interest in being the biggest.

We're a small studio on purpose. Every project gets the same four hands on it from first sketch to final file, no handoffs, no account managers relaying notes. Founded in Lahore in 2019, we now work with makers and small brands across three continents.

— How a project runs

Three stages, same as firing clay.

Listen

Two weeks understanding the brand as it actually is today, not as a mood board. We sit with the founders, the product, the customers.

Shape

Concepts, refinement, one direction chosen together. This is where most of the back-and-forth lives, and where we're slowest on purpose.

Fire

Final files, motion, launch assets. Once it's fired it's fixed, so nothing reaches this stage until everyone's sure.
